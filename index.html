About

Applied mathematics through rigorous numerical methods

I work on numerical methods for differential equations and mathematical models arising in continuum mechanics and wave propagation. My recent work studies stable and convergent multi-layer time discretizations for nonlinear evolution problems, including Kirchhoff-type string equations and coupled hyperbolic systems related to the Timoshenko beam model.

Current publications also include spectral approximation with Legendre polynomials, operator-based approximation techniques, and numerical analysis of wave propagation in discrete lattices. Recent papers list affiliations with The University of Georgia and the Ilia Vekua Institute of Applied Mathematics in Tbilisi.

Research

Research themes

01

Numerical analysis of evolution equations

Stability, convergence, error estimates, and constructive approximation schemes for nonlinear evolution problems.

02

Hyperbolic PDEs & nonlinear dynamics

Computational treatment of Kirchhoff-type strings, Timoshenko-type systems, and related models in mathematical physics.

03

Spectral & high-order methods

Legendre-Galerkin and polynomial approximation techniques for efficient spatial discretization.

04

Wave propagation

Analytical and numerical study of waves in discrete media, including finite sources in infinite triangular lattices.
